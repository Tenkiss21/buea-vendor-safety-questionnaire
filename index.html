Accident and health risks among informal petroleum vendors

A research questionnaire for vendors working in Buea Municipality.

This study explores work conditions, accident experiences, health concerns, safety practices and emergency preparedness among informal vendors of petroleum products. Health questions describe reported symptoms and do not establish that work exposure caused them.

No name is requested. At the end, you can download a PDF and email it to the researcher. Automatic delivery is available when configured; otherwise, you can share the PDF or open an email draft.

Participation is voluntary. Do not enter names or other identifying details in open-text answers. You may leave any question unanswered. The symptom questions are for research purposes and are not a medical assessment.
